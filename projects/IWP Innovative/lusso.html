Design Philosophy

The Lusso V10 embodies the purest expression of Italian grand touring — where design becomes emotion, and luxury becomes performance. Every surface flows with intent, every contour captures the light as if in motion. It is not merely styled; it is crafted, with precision born from passion. From its sweeping silhouette to the muscular stance that anchors it to the road, the Lusso V10 speaks a language of balance — strength refined by grace. Its proportions are timeless, inspired by the golden age of grand tourers, yet sharpened by modern aerodynamics and carbon mastery. Inside, the philosophy continues: simplicity as sophistication. The cockpit surrounds the driver in an atmosphere of tailored luxury, where hand-stitched leather, brushed aluminum, and digital elegance coexist in perfect harmony.
